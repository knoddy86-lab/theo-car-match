# Hooray library — credits and licences

These clips play after each matched pair in Theo's Car Match, with the wiggle and confetti. All four are **real recordings** from [Freesound](https://freesound.org/), licensed **CC0 1.0** (Creative Commons Zero, public domain dedication). Each sound's licence was confirmed on its Freesound page (`creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero`).

Clips were trimmed to under about 1.5 seconds, faded in and out, loudness-normalised to about −19 LUFS (a little quieter than the round-end V8 revs), and encoded as mono MP3 at 96 kbps. They rotate so the cheer is not the same every time.
