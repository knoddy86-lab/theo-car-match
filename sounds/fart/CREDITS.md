# Fart library — credits and licences

These clips play in Name Game when a wrong car is tapped (the card pops away). All are **real recordings** from [Freesound](https://freesound.org/), licensed **CC0 1.0** (Creative Commons Zero, public domain dedication). Each sound's licence was confirmed on its Freesound page (`creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero`).

Clips were faded, loudness-normalised to about −19 LUFS (the shortest clip is peak-limited and a little quieter), and encoded as mono MP3 at 96 kbps. They rotate so the same sound is not played twice in a row.
