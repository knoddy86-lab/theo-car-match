# V8 rev library — credits and licences

These clips play when a round of Theo's Car Match is finished. All twelve were **synthesized on the box** for this project (original computer-generated audio, not recordings of a real car).

I looked for clearly licensed real V8 recordings (Freesound CC0, Pixabay, Wikimedia Commons) that could be downloaded **without logging in**. Freesound full-file downloads redirected to a login page, so no third-party recordings were included. The library is therefore entirely original synthesis, free to redistribute with the game.

## Method

Each file is a mono MP3 at about 80 kbps, 1.5–3 seconds long, peak near −6 dBFS, with a short fade in and out. They were generated with a cross-plane V8 model: firing order 1-8-4-3-6-5-7-2, pulses every 90° of crank, two-bank spacing for the classic burble, RPM envelopes, harmonics, exhaust-style filtering, light mechanical noise, and optional decel crackle.
