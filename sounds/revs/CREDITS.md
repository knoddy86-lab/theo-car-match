# V8 rev library — credits and licences

These clips play when a round of Theo's Car Match is finished. All twelve are **real V8 engine recordings** from [Freesound](https://freesound.org/), licensed **CC0 1.0** (Creative Commons Zero, public domain dedication). Each sound's licence was confirmed on its Freesound page (`creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero`).

Clips were trimmed to the best 2–4 second rev, faded in and out, loudness-normalised to about −16 LUFS, and encoded as mono MP3 at 128 kbps.
