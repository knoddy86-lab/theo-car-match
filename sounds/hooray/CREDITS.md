# Hooray library — credits and licences

These clips play after each matched pair in Theo's Car Match, with the wiggle and confetti. All are **real recordings** from [Freesound](https://freesound.org/), licensed **CC0 1.0** (Creative Commons Zero, public domain dedication). Each sound's licence was confirmed on its Freesound page (`creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero`).

Clips were trimmed to a short cheer, faded in and out, loudness-normalised to about −19 LUFS (a little quieter than the round-end V8 revs), and encoded as mono MP3 at 96 kbps. They rotate so the same cheer is not played twice in a row.

CC0 recordings of children clearly saying the English word “hooray” are scarce (several otherwise-perfect kid “hooray” clips are CC BY, not CC0, so they are not used). This pack therefore keeps only one short “yay” and one “woo hoo”, and adds every CC0 clip we could confirm actually says “hooray” — including two short “hip hip hooray” phrases from a small group, which was the last-resort option.
