# Classic line clips

v41: uncle-ashley, aunty-ashleigh, wheres-uncle-ashley, wheres-aunty-ashleigh. Made with Sound of Text (Google, en-AU), the same voice and service as the original Classic car-name clips. The game plays them for these lines when a part of the game is set to the Classic voice; any other Classic line uses the device voice.

v45: every clip in the app was loudness-matched to about -16 LUFS integrated with true peak at or below -1.5 dBTP (gain plus a gentle peak limiter, re-encoded at the same sample rate and bitrate; content, length and licence unchanged). Before, levels ranged from about -23 to -15 LUFS.
v45: berry, moss, dart, rosie, racer, officer, dot (Name game car names said as first/last name, and the three cars that had no Classic clip). Made with Sound of Text (Google, en-AU), the same voice and service as the other Classic clips; trimmed and loudness-matched. These replace the quieter device voice for those words.
v46: find, then, whos-missing, look-carefully, super-fast (Name game harder tricks). Made with Sound of Text (Google, en-AU), same voice and service; loudness-matched to -16 LUFS / -1.5 dBTP.
