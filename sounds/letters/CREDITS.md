# Letters voice (v61)

The 26 letter-name clips `let-a.mp3` to `let-z.mp3` (British English letter names: H "aitch", W "double you", Z "zed").

These are the clips the Letters game plays by default, and also when the Alba letters voice is chosen. Classic stays on `sounds/classic/`.

- **L, I and R:** Piper `en_GB-cori-high` (Cori, UK English, female). Training recordings from [LibriVox](https://librivox.org), **public domain**. The model was trained from scratch by [Bryce Beattie](https://brycebeattie.com/files/tts/). Credit: *Voice: Piper "Cori" (en_GB-cori-high), public domain LibriVox recordings.*
- **The other 23 letters (A–K, M–Q, S–Z except I and L and R):** Piper `en_GB-alba-medium` ("Alba", Scottish English, female). Voice data from the University of Edinburgh CSTR, **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC BY 4.0)** (https://datashare.ed.ac.uk/handle/10283/3270). Engine: Piper (MIT licence). Credit: *Voice: "Alba" by the Centre for Speech Technology Research, University of Edinburgh (CC BY 4.0), via the Piper en_GB-alba-medium model.*

v61 replaced the earlier Kokoro Emma set (`sounds/letters-emma/`), which had lead-in and pronunciation problems, and the Alba-only L, I and R, which sounded too Scottish.

**Levelling and format:** about −19.2 LUFS integrated, true peak −4.6 dBFS or lower; mono MP3, 22.05 kHz, 40 kbit/s (same as the Numbers Alba clips).
